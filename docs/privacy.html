DareDash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

DareDash is a pass-and-play party game. It does not require an account. Names added to the guest player list and game scores are handled locally on your device. You may choose to share a player list through another app; names are shared only when you choose that action. Party games do not synchronize across devices.

Advertising

The Android app includes Google AdMob advertising software and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. Depending on your region and choices, Google and its partners may collect or process advertising identifiers, IP-based general location, device information, app/ad usage and diagnostics in order to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Read Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps. Ads may be unavailable while consent or app approval is pending.

Purchases

Optional paid upgrades are not currently offered in the launch build. If later activated, Google Play will handle payments; future privacy notices will describe any server-side purchase verification.

Data sharing and retention

Guest player names remain on the device until removed in the app or its application data is cleared. Your Android sharing sheet can transmit a message or roster to the recipient you choose. Advertising providers may retain information under their own policies.

Permissions

DareDash uses network connectivity for ads and potential Google Play services. It does not request access to camera, contacts, microphone or precise device location.

Children

This app is not designed specifically for children under 13. Users should skip challenges they do not wish to complete. Advertising and target audience disclosures will be configured according to Google Play's policies.

Contact

For app support and privacy questions, use the support page. Before public launch, the developer must add a monitored support email address and verify that this notice accurately reflects deployed SDK behavior.
