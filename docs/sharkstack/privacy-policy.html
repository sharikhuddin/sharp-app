SharkStack Splash — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026. This is a draft policy that must be reviewed and updated with the publisher's legal/contact information and final product configuration before public release.

Information saved on your device

The game stores your puzzle progress, high score, pearl balance and sound preference on your device, together with daily-reward history, optional Shark Crew nickname, random friend code, local referral-confirmation history, and transaction-delivery IDs where applicable. Puzzle progress generally stays on the device. Account details, if enabled, are handled separately as explained below.

Optional score and friend sharing

You may choose to send a score or friend invitation using your device's standard share menu. The app does not automatically post to, log in to, or access your social media accounts, contacts or messages. Choosing a third-party app to share through transfers the message to that service under its own privacy practices. Offline invitations are stored locally and unverified; connected accounts may earn server-approved virtual rewards.

Optional verified Shark Crew account

If enabled, email sign-in uses one-time codes. A dedicated Supabase account database may store an email address, account identifier, nickname, friend code, referral relationships, and the virtual reward delivery history. These details support sign-in, limit duplicate bonus claims, and deliver earned Shark Pearls. Signing in is optional and the puzzle stays playable offline. SharkStack does not access social-media contacts or post automatically. A referral verifies an account and email, not a distinct device or physical installation.

The publisher must provide a support contact and honor data deletion requests. Before public release, replace any placeholder text and confirm the actual data handling.

Advertisements

The Android version integrates Google's AdMob SDK. AdMob and its partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network data, ad interactions and diagnostics to serve, measure and protect ads, subject to user choices, applicable law, and Google's policies. See Google's advertising information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional Google Play purchases

If enabled, optional consumable Shark Pearl purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google manages payment details and provides the app with purchase status and a transaction token; SharkStack does not ask you for card information. The Android app uses local purchase-delivery records to prevent duplicate pearl grants. Purchases are optional and game pearls have no real-world cash value.

Your choices

The Android app requests consent where required before loading ads. Ad privacy choices can be accessed from the in-game settings when available. Rewarded video ads are optional; players can start a new game without viewing any ad.

Data sharing and retention

Optional verified account data is stored only after the publisher configures and enables a dedicated account backend. Local game progress persists on your device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Advertising data handling is controlled by Google's advertising services and applicable settings.

Children

This game is not specifically designed or marketed for children. The publisher must configure audience declarations and ads settings in Play Console and AdMob based on the actual intended audience.

Delete a verified account

If online Shark Crew is enabled, you can delete your verified account inside the app: Shark Crew → Delete account. Deleting an account removes its profile, server-side friend code, referrals and associated virtual referral reward history. Locally stored game progress may remain until you clear app data. You can also request deletion using the publisher’s external account-deletion request page when it is published.
