YapThing Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026
Developer: Shark Corp
Contact: Sharkcorp001@gmail.com

Local text AI and conversations

YapThing runs its bundled text AI on your device. Questions and replies are not sent to a cloud AI provider. The model cannot understand uploaded images or access live internet information.

Text conversations and preferences stay in app storage on your device. Selected photos are temporary and are not uploaded. You can delete saved chats in the app; uninstalling removes local app data. Exports and shares go only to the apps or destinations you choose.

Optional reports

If you choose Send report, YapThing sends the selected AI answer, your reason, and the app version over HTTPS to our Supabase reporting database hosted in the United States. Please do not include private information in reports. Hosting services may process connection metadata, such as IP addresses, for network operation and abuse prevention. Reports are restricted to the developer for safety review and support.

Reports are retained until reviewed and no longer needed for safety or legal purposes. Contact us to request deletion, describing the report and approximate submission time; we may need information to identify it.

Optional purchases and analytics

Google Play processes optional purchases under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive payment-card details. Plus purchases remain unavailable until the product is activated. No advertising or analytics SDK is included in the edition described here.

Data and safety

We do not sell personal data. The Android app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. AI answers may be inaccurate; do not rely on them for emergencies or professional advice.

Website hosting

This informational website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses and technical metadata in accordance with its privacy policies. The website does not run the YapThing AI model or process your Android text chats. This website uses no advertising or analytics code.

Contact and changes

We may update this notice as app features change. Contact Sharkcorp001@gmail.com for privacy and support requests.
