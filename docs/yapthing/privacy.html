Accounts and sign-in

YapThing supports optional account registration with an email address and password through Supabase Auth (United States region). Account passwords are sent to Supabase over HTTPS and are not stored as plaintext by YapThing. An encrypted session refresh token is stored within the Android app using Android Keystore; users can sign out, and their local conversations and memories are separated by account and by Personal/Work space. Creating an account does not yet synchronize chat history between devices. Supabase and network providers may process account identifiers, authentication events and connection metadata necessary to deliver sign-in.

Google, GitHub, Facebook, Microsoft and Apple sign-in appear as optional choices but are disabled until the respective provider is configured and approved; none currently access a user's social-media account. If activated in a future release, authentication data will be handled according to the provider's authorization flow. Social sign-in is separate from granting YapThing access to Gmail inboxes, Drive documents or other account content.

Accounts do not currently synchronize chat messages or memories across devices. Personal and Work chat storage is divided by account ID on the local device; people with access to the same unlocked device may still be able to access local app data. Users can use local AI as guests. To request an account deletion, contact Sharkcorp001@gmail.com; deleting an account does not automatically remove every locally exported file.

Local text AI and conversations

YapThing runs its bundled text AI on your device. Questions and replies are not sent to a cloud AI provider. The compact text model does not directly understand image pixels or independently browse the open web. Optional YapSearch imports selected Wikipedia article excerpts as reference context, and native on-device image recognition can extract readable text and broad image labels.

Personal and Work YapSpaces store separate conversations in local app storage. YapMemory saves user-entered facts or optional automatically recognized simple preferences; automatic memory is off by default. You can inspect or erase saved facts and chats in the app. Documents and selected photos are processed locally, and the document reader extracts a limited amount of text. Images are not uploaded by YapThing's OCR and labeling feature. You can delete saved chats in the app; uninstalling normally removes local app data. Exports and shares go only to the apps or destinations you choose.

YapSearch (optional)

When you explicitly press Search, the Android app sends the query to your chosen public search source: Wikipedia or GitHub's public repository-search API, over HTTPS. Wikipedia, GitHub, and their network services may process the query, IP address and technical request data under their own privacy practices. YapThing displays source links and can pass the user-selected article excerpts into the on-device text model. This edition does not search your Gmail, Google Drive, Slack or other accounts.

Optional reports

If you choose Send report, YapThing sends the selected AI answer, your reason, and the app version over HTTPS to our Supabase reporting database hosted in the United States. Please do not include private information in reports. Hosting services may process connection metadata, such as IP addresses, for network operation and abuse prevention. Reports are restricted to the developer for safety review and support.

Reports are retained until reviewed and no longer needed for safety or legal purposes. Contact us to request deletion, describing the report and approximate submission time; we may need information to identify it.

Optional purchases and analytics

If activated in a later release, Google Play processes optional purchases under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive payment-card details. Plus purchases remain unavailable until the product is activated. No advertising or analytics SDK is included in the edition described here.

Data and safety

We do not sell personal data. The Android app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. AI answers may be inaccurate; do not rely on them for emergencies or professional advice.

Website hosting

This informational website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses and technical metadata in accordance with its privacy policies. The website does not run the YapThing AI model or process your Android text chats. This website uses no advertising or analytics code.

Contact and changes

We may update this notice as app features change. Contact Sharkcorp001@gmail.com for privacy and support requests.
