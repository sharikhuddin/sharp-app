Meet your YapThing

🔐 YapThing Account

Create an optional account with email and password to keep Personal and Work Yaps separate on your Android device. Google and other social sign-in providers still require setup; cloud chat synchronization isn't available yet.

✳ YapSpace

Separate Personal and Work conversations on your device, plus quick new chats and characters with personality.

▤ YapVault

Find, revisit, rename and delete saved conversations instead of starting over.

🧠 YapMemory

Save preferences to personalize later chats. Optional automatic saving of simple preferences is off by default, and you control deletion.

📄 YapTools

Bring text documents, selected PDFs and images into a chat. On-device image features extract text and broad labels rather than offering full photo understanding.

⌕ YapSearch

Look up Wikipedia articles and prepare a cited response with the local AI. Wider web search and live news sources aren't enabled yet.

◈ YapLinks

One place for future Gmail, Drive, Outlook and Slack connections. Live syncing isn't available yet; manual imports work without sharing account passwords.

Your Yap. Your rules.

The included compact AI model generates replies on your phone. Conversation history and YapMemory are saved locally and separated by YapSpace. Optional user-initiated reports and YapSearch requests use the internet as described in our privacy policy.
