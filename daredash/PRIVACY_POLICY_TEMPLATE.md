DareDash is an offline party game. We do not require user registration or collect player names on our servers. Names and game scores entered during a play session remain locally on the device. Purchase entitlement state is stored locally so purchases can be restored through Google Play.

For advertising, the app uses Google AdMob. When production advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics in accordance with Google's policies and consent settings. Advertising can be personalized or non-personalized depending on the user's choices and applicable law. Read [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

For optional in-app purchases, Google Play processes payment information; the developer does not directly receive payment-card details. Google's systems provide purchase and entitlement details needed to enable features. Before launching server-side purchase verification, update this policy to describe any backend and data retention.

The app does not request camera, contacts, microphone, or precise location access. Some service providers may process network or device information described above.

Data retention: Local settings and cached purchase entitlements remain until app data is removed or the app is uninstalled. Third-party providers follow their own retention policies. Contact the address above for privacy requests. Since no account is created in the app, provide a process for handling requests from users appropriately.

Children: The product is planned for a general audience; do not advertise it as child-directed without implementing Google's Families and child-related advertising requirements. Parents should supervise gameplay where appropriate.
