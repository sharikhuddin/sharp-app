# DareDash — draft Google Play listing

**App name:** DareDash: Party Challenges

**Short description:** Truth, dares and fun challenges with up to 24 friends. Play anywhere!

**Full description:**

Bring game night to life with DareDash! Gather your friends and pass one phone around for laughs, creative challenges and friendly competition.

Add up to 24 players by name, choose from five free categories, and race a 30-second timer as you take turns. Complete a challenge to score points or pass when you'd rather skip. Share your results when the party ends!

Features:
- 120 free Truth, Dare, Would You Rather, Most Likely To and Icebreaker prompts
- Mixed mode to keep things unexpected
- 2–24 players, easy bulk add and saved local guest lists
- Party invitations through WhatsApp, SMS and other apps
- Turn-by-turn scoring, 30-second countdown, leaderboards and winners
- Offline party play; no registration or login required
- Optional advertising-supported free version with consent choices

DareDash currently supports **pass-and-play on the same phone**, not synchronized internet multiplayer. An invitation can encourage friends to join your in-person party or install the app. Premium challenges are planned for a later release.

Play responsibly; participants can always pass on a challenge.

**Review before submission:** Do not claim 24-player multi-device sessions, live premium purchases, ad approval or features not tested on device. Use only real gameplay screenshots. Verify store listing name trademark availability and target audience/content rating; final Play classification depends on Google's questionnaire.

**Store creative files required:** 512×512 PNG icon (<=1MB), 1024×500 JPG/PNG feature graphic, >=2 real phone screenshots following Google Play sizing guidance. Current assets must be created from the actual app or designer-approved visuals; this commit does not invent screenshots.

**Data safety:** Complete based on the actual Google Mobile Ads SDK and consent configuration and released behavior. Do not select 'collects no data' simply because player names are local; AdMob may collect device/ad identifiers and diagnostics.
