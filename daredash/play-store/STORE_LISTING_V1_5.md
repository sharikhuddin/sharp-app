**App name:** DareDash: Party Challenges

**Short description (within 80 characters):** Truth or dare, quick party questions and laughs with friends. Play offline!

**Full description:**

Make every hangout a game night with DareDash, a lively pass-and-play party game for your whole group.

Pick a category, pass the phone, and see who is next! DareDash includes 120 free prompts across Truth, Dare, Would You Rather, Most Likely To, and Icebreakers. Race against the 30-second clock or pass on any challenge.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Invite friends via WhatsApp, SMS or your favorite sharing app.
• Add 2 to 24 player names, individually or all at once.
• Shuffle the player order for a fresh round.
• Earn points, see the leaderboard and share the winners.
• Keep your guest list saved on your phone.
• Enjoy offline party play without creating an account.

DareDash supports playing together around **one phone**. It does not provide synchronized multiplayer rooms across devices. The free app includes optional ads subject to consent and availability. Premium purchases are not yet active.

All challenges are optional; players can always pass.
